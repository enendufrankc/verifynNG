# Console guide: Settings

Organization details, security, and integrations live here across three tabs:

- **Organization** — your business's name, branding, and contact details as
  shown on verify pages and in emails to consumers.
- **Security** — active sessions, and (once you've set it up) multi-factor
  authentication.
- **API keys** — programmatic access to your tenant's data (owner only).

Changing your organization's legal name or trademark details may require
re-verification — see [How codes work](/docs/codes) for why identity checks
matter to this platform. Anything else here takes effect immediately.
