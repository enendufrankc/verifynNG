# Console guide: Team

Manage who has access to your organization's console and what they can do.

| Role         | Can do                                                                                                                                       |
| ------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Viewer**   | Read everything — batches, units, scans, reports, analytics. No changes.                                                                     |
| **Operator** | Everything a viewer can, plus day-to-day actions: flag units, work reports and anomalies, manage products and OEMs.                          |
| **Owner**    | Everything an operator can, plus billing, team management, and anything irreversible (minting, decommissioning units, deleting the account). |

Only an owner can invite members or change roles. Removing someone revokes
their access immediately — there's no grace period.

If someone's access looks wrong and you can't fix it as an owner (a locked
account, a role that won't save), that's a [support ticket](/console/help).
