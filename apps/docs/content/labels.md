# Applying labels

## Tier 1 (on the pack)

The Tier-1 QR goes on the outside of the container, wherever a shopper would
naturally look — usually the front or back panel, near the batch/lot code.
It must be readable at a normal arm's-length scanning distance under
ordinary shop lighting; test it with a mid-range phone camera, not just a
dedicated scanner.

## Tier 2 (scratch-off / under-cap / under-seal)

Tier 2 has one hard rule: **it must be genuinely unreadable before the
product is opened or the seal is broken.** Three common placements, in order
of how tamper-evident they are:

1. **Scratch-off panel** (like a lottery ticket) applied inside the box or
   under the outer wrap. Cheapest to retrofit onto existing packaging.
2. **Under the cap** — printed on the inside of a screw cap or the foil
   under it. Requires the cap/foil supplier to print, not just the label
   supplier.
3. **Under a tamper-evident seal** on the container itself.

Whichever placement you use, verify two things before a production run:

- **It really is hidden.** Hold a finished sample up to a bright light and a
  phone flashlight — some scratch-off inks are thinner than they look under
  normal light.
- **It survives normal handling.** A code that partially rubs off in
  transit produces "code not found" reports that look like counterfeits but
  aren't.

## Before you print at volume

Run the [printer & label specs](/docs/printers) checklist against a small
test batch first. It is much cheaper to catch a bad module size or a wrong
error-correction level on 50 test labels than on 50,000 production ones.
